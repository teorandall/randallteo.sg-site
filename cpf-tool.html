What the three sums mean

Basic Retirement Sum

The amount CPF estimates covers basic living needs in retirement, assuming you own a property you can live in and pay no rent. You can set aside the BRS instead of the FRS if you pledge that property.

Full Retirement Sum

Exactly double the BRS. This is the default amount transferred into your Retirement Account at 55, and the reference point CPF uses for withdrawal rules. Savings above it can generally be withdrawn from 55.

Enhanced Retirement Sum

The ceiling you may voluntarily top up to for higher payouts. Since 2025 it has been four times the BRS. It is the one sum that does not lock to your cohort.

The distinction that catches most people out. Your BRS and FRS are fixed in the year you turn 55 and never change again. The ERS is not tied to your age or your cohort at all — it rises every 1 January and applies to everyone aged 55 and above in that year. So if you turned 55 some years ago, your BRS and FRS come from your own row below, but your ERS is the one on this year's row.
